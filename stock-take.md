# Stock Take

Fill this in once, before the first order, and again whenever the cupboard
drifts from what `stock.md` says. Rough amounts are fine ("half a bag", "about
1 kg", "low"). Leave a line blank if you have none.

Only Infinity and Ocado items are listed. Fresh food is bought weekly, so it
isn't counted.

When it's done, tell Claude Code "stock take done" (or paste your answers, or
send photos of the shelves). It turns this into `stock.md` in proper units and
re-checks this month's order against it.
