## Burnt Aubergine Veggie Chilli
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, freezer-friendly
Source: bbcgoodfood.com

Ingredients:
- 1 aubergine
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 red onion (diced)
- 2 carrot (finely diced)
- 70 g green lentils (rinsed)
- 30 g red lentils (rinsed)
- 1 can kidney beans (with liquid)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 20 g dark chocolate (finely chopped)
- 0.25 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 800 ml vegetable stock
- 0.5 lime (juiced)
- 300 g brown rice (to serve)
- to taste salt

Notes: Char the aubergine whole on a gas ring (or under a hot grill) until
blackened, peel and chop. Fry onion and carrots 15-20 min, add everything
else, simmer covered 1½ hrs, then uncovered 15 min. Stir in lime. Optional
toppings: tortilla chips, avocado, vegan yoghurt, fresh coriander.

## Cauliflower Shawarma with Pomegranate, Pine Nuts and Rose
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, sunday roast
Source: thehappyfoodie.co.uk

Ingredients:
- 40 g vegan butter (softened)
- 1 lemon (juice for the butter and 1 tbsp for the tahini sauce)
- 2 clove garlic (1 for butter, 1 for tahini sauce)
- 1.5 tbsp fresh coriander (finely chopped)
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp sumac
- 1.5 tsp ground cumin
- 0.5 tsp ground allspice
- pinch ground nutmeg
- pinch ground cardamom
- 1 cauliflower
- 1 tbsp pomegranate molasses
- 1.5 tbsp pine nuts (toasted)
- 1 green chilli (finely chopped)
- 1 pomegranate (2 tbsp seeds)
- 1 tsp dried rose petals
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley (roughly chopped)
- 100 g tahini
- to taste salt

Notes: Beat the spices, lemon juice, garlic and coriander into the butter.
Boil the whole cauliflower ~7 min until al dente, drain, brush with the
spiced butter, then roast at 240°C for 5-7 min until charred. Tahini sauce:
whisk tahini with lemon, garlic and ~100 ml iced water. Spoon sauce and
molasses over, then scatter the garnishes. Serves 4 as a main, up to 8 as
a side.

## Chilli-Charred Brussels Sprouts
Servings: 6
Tags: side
Source: bbcgoodfood.com

Ingredients:
- 600 g brussels sprouts
- 60 ml olive oil
- 4 clove garlic (bashed)
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 lemon (zested and juiced)
- to taste salt

Notes: Boil sprouts 4-5 min, halve. Fry garlic in 3 tbsp oil until golden,
discard it, add chilli and sprouts cut-side down for 10 min. Add the rest
of the oil and lemon juice. Holds in a low oven for 20 min. No chilli?
Use flaked almonds and black pepper.

## Creamy Leek Pasta
Servings: 2
Tags: dinner, quick, freezer-friendly
Source: oddbox.co.uk

Ingredients:
- 2 leek (thinly sliced)
- 1 tbsp vegan butter
- 125 ml white wine (optional, or vegetable stock)
- 1 lemon (zest and juice)
- 100 g vegan crème fraîche
- 1 tsp wholegrain mustard
- 20 g vegan parmesan (grated)
- 200 g spaghetti
- to taste salt
- to taste black pepper

Notes: Soften leeks in butter 5 min, add wine, crème fraîche and mustard,
cook 10 min until creamy. Toss with pasta, a splash of pasta water, the
parmesan, lemon. Keeps 3 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.

## Lentil Bolognese
Servings: 12
Tags: dinner, freezer-friendly, batch
Source: rainbowplantlife.com

Ingredients:
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 onion (diced)
- 8 clove garlic (minced)
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp dried thyme
- 300 g tomato purée
- 240 ml red wine (optional)
- 1.44 l vegetable stock
- 370 g red lentils (soaked 30 min)
- 64 g walnuts (blitzed finely)
- 2 can chopped tomatoes
- 800 g pasta (tagliatelle, rigatoni or penne)
- 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley (or basil)
- to taste salt
- to taste black pepper

Notes: Brown onions slowly, add garlic and herbs, then caramelise the
tomato purée. Deglaze with wine, add stock, lentils and walnuts, simmer
20 min, then tomatoes for 15-20 min more. Finish with balsamic. Freeze the
sauce in portions without the pasta. Check the pasta is egg-free.

## Roasted Vegetable Lasagne
Servings: 6
Tags: dinner, freezer-friendly
Source: nutritionfacts.org

Ingredients:
- 1 cauliflower (sliced 5 mm thick)
- 1 courgette (thinly sliced)
- 1 aubergine (thinly sliced)
- 1 red pepper (chopped)
- 250 g wholewheat lasagne sheets (about 12)
- 1 can cannellini beans (drained and mashed)
- 4 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 4 tbsp fresh parsley (minced)
- 120 ml plant milk
- 0.25 lemon (1 tsp juice)
- 1 tsp white miso
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 0.25 tsp chilli flakes
- 0.25 tsp black pepper
- 700 g passata (or marinara sauce)
- 2 tbsp walnuts (for the nutty parm)
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast (for the nutty parm)

Notes: Roast the veg at 210°C ~20 min. Blitz the cauliflower and mix with
the beans, yeast, milk and seasonings. Layer sauce, sheets, veg and
cauliflower mix twice, finishing with sheets and sauce. Top with nutty
parm (walnuts blitzed with the yeast) and bake covered at 175°C for 30-40 min. Our notes: cashew cream on
top works well; try soya mince next time; burnt aubergine recommended.

## Fennel and Parsnip Tarka Dal
Servings: 4
Tags: dinner, freezer-friendly
Source: Sasha Gill, worcesternews.co.uk

Ingredients:
- 200 g red lentils
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 l vegetable stock
- 1 parsnip (cut into batons)
- 2 fennel (diced)
- 2 tsp vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp cumin seeds (plus 1 tsp for roasting)
- 75 g fresh coriander (leaves only)
- 35 g flaked almonds (toasted)
- 1 tbsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
- pinch asafoetida (optional)
- 1 red onion (finely chopped)
- 5 cm fresh ginger (finely chopped)
- 2 green chilli (deseeded and chopped)
- 2 tomato (diced)
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 300 g brown rice (to serve, or naan/chapattis)
- to taste salt

Notes: Simmer lentils, turmeric, bay and stock 30 min. Roast fennel and
parsnip with oil, 1 tsp cumin seeds and a little salt at 200°C for
25-30 min. Fry the mustard, fenugreek, cumin seeds and asafoetida until
they pop, add onion, ginger, chillies, then tomatoes and chilli powder.
Stir it all into the dal with the roast veg; top with coriander and
almonds.

veg, mayo and the extra sauce.

## The Best Crispy Roast Potatoes
Servings: 6
Tags: side, sunday roast
Source: seriouseats.com

Ingredients:
- 2 kg potatoes (Maris Piper or Yukon Gold, chunks)
- 0.5 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 75 ml olive oil
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary (finely chopped)
- 5 clove garlic (minced)
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley (minced)
- to taste salt
- to taste black pepper

Notes: Boil the potatoes ~10 min in salted water with the bicarb. Heat the
oil with rosemary, garlic and pepper until the garlic just colours, then
strain (keep both). Toss the potatoes hard in the oil to rough them up,
roast at 200°C for 20 min, then turn and roast 30-40 min more. Toss with
the garlic, rosemary and parsley.

## Vegan Mac and Cheese
Servings: 8
Tags: dinner, comfort
Source: noracooks.com

Ingredients:
- 210 g cashews
- 480 ml water
- 1 lemon (3 tbsp juice)
- 30 g nutritional yeast
- 0.25 tsp turmeric
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 200 g vegan cheddar (grated, optional)
- 340 g macaroni
- 75 g panko breadcrumbs (optional topping)
- 4 tbsp vegan butter (melted, for topping)
- 0.25 tsp smoked paprika
- to taste salt

Notes: Soak cashews in boiling water 5 min, drain, then blend with the
water, lemon, yeast, spices and cheese until smooth. Stir through cooked
pasta. Best baked: top with the breadcrumbs, butter and paprika and bake
at 175°C for 15 min, then grill until golden.
