# Meal Plan: October–November 2026 (draft)

Four weeks from Sunday 11 October. Quantities are for two adults and our
daughter (about 2.5 portions a meal), plus 2–3 guests on Saturdays.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Dinner | Lunch next day |
|-----|--------|----------------|
| Sun | **Freezer batch** (~16 portions): eat 3 meals, freeze 3 family bags | Mon lunch + Mon dinner from the same pot |
| Tue | **Batch** (~10 portions) | Wed lunch + dinner, Thu lunch |
| Thu | **Small cook** (~6 portions) | Fri lunch |
| Fri | From the freezer (take out Thursday night) | Sat lunch: also freezer |
| Sat | **Guests** (~8–9 portions) | Sun lunch |

Every day: Berry & Flax Porridge on weekdays; Baked Carrot Cake Oatmeal on
Saturdays and Tofu Scramble on Sundays. Three pieces of fruit each, hummus
with carrot sticks, and Garlicky Greens with three dinners a week (two with
fresh kale, one with frozen broccoli).

## 🧊 Freezer plan

One family bag is a meal for all three of us. A lunch tub is one adult
portion. Eat the oldest first.

| Week | In | Out | In the freezer after |
|------|----|-----|----------------------|
| 1 | 3 bags bolognese sauce, 8 tubs carrot soup | Fri: 1 bag bolognese. Sat lunch: 3 tubs soup | 2 bolognese, 5 tubs |
| 2 | 3 bags chilli | Fri: 1 bag bolognese. Sat lunch: 3 tubs soup | 1 bolognese, 3 chilli, 2 tubs |
| 3 | 3 bags black bean stew | Fri: 1 bag bolognese. Sat lunch: 1 bag chilli | 2 chilli, 3 black bean, 2 tubs |
| 4 | 3 bags Tuscan soup | Fri: 1 bag chilli. Sat lunch: 1 bag black bean | 1 chilli, 2 black bean, 3 Tuscan, 2 tubs |

The month ends with 6 family meals and 2 lunch tubs in the freezer. That's a
buffer for a busy or poorly week, or a night off. Next month, make one Sunday
a normal-sized cook so the freezer doesn't keep growing.

You'll need about 12 freezer bags or boxes that hold 3 portions each, and 8
single lunch tubs. Label each with the meal and date.

## 🧺 Bulk fresh order (start of month)

Hardy veg and fruit for the whole month, with about 10% spare. About 45 kg in
all.

- [ ] **Apples (or pears)**: 14 kg, about 95. Store in the fridge or a cold
  room; they keep 4+ weeks. *(daily fruit, chickpea salad)*
- [ ] **Satsumas**: 5 kg, bought in two lots of 2.5 kg (weeks 1 and 3).
  Store in the fridge. *(daily fruit)*
- [ ] **Carrots**: 8 kg. Store in the fridge drawer or a cold garage.
  *(soup, chilli, dal, stews, slaw, snacks)* A 10 kg sack is fine: make a
  second pot of carrot soup with the spare.
- [ ] **Sweet potatoes**: 6 kg. Store in a cool room, not the fridge.
  *(enchiladas, dal, black bean stew, quinoa stew, salad)*
- [ ] **Onions**: 3 kg. Store somewhere cool, dark and airy.
- [ ] **Red onions**: 2.5 kg. Store with the onions.
- [ ] **Potatoes**: 3 kg. Store cool and dark. *(all for the week 4 roast:
  buy them that week if you'd rather)*
- [ ] **Garlic**: 12 bulbs. Store cool and dry.
- [ ] **Lemons**: 24. Store in the fridge.
- [ ] **Red cabbage**: 2 heads. Store whole in the fridge. *(slaw in weeks
  1 and 3)*
- [ ] **Fresh ginger**: 150 g. Freeze it and grate from frozen.

Also buy these in bulk on the monthly Ocado order: **4 kg frozen berries**
(porridge), **1.6 kg frozen broccoli** (greens), 500 g frozen spinach, 400 g
frozen peas and 300 g sweetcorn.

## Daily Dozen check

- **Beans:** every lunch and dinner, plus hummus.
- **Berries, flaxseed, nuts and a whole grain:** the porridge.
- **Other fruit:** three pieces each from the apples, satsumas and bananas.
- **Greens and cruciferous veg:** kale or broccoli on three nights, plus
  the slaw, sprouts, cauliflower and pak choi. Add a handful of spinach or
  rocket to lunches to reach two servings of greens a day.
- **B12:** Greger recommends a supplement for adults. The NHS recommends
  vitamin drops for under-5s.
