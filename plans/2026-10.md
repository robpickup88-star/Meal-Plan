# Meal Plan: October–November 2026 (draft)

Four weeks from Sunday 11 October. Quantities are for two adults and our
daughter (about 2.5 portions a meal), plus 2–3 guests on Saturdays. The
plan follows the season: the last aubergines and courgettes in weeks 1–2,
then squash, roots, cauliflower, kale and sprouts as November arrives.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Dinner | Covers |
|-----|--------|--------|
| Sun | **Freezer batch** (~16 portions) | Sun dinner, Mon lunch and dinner, plus 3 family bags to freeze |
| Tue | **Batch** (~10 portions) | Tue dinner, Wed lunch and dinner, Thu lunch |
| Thu | **Small cook** (~6 portions) | Thu dinner, Fri lunch |
| Fri | From the freezer (take out Thursday night) | Fri dinner, Sat lunch |
| Sat | **Guests** (~8–9 portions) | Sat dinner, Sun lunch |

Every day: Berry & Flax Porridge on weekdays; Baked Carrot Cake Oatmeal on
Saturdays and Tofu Scramble on Sundays. Three pieces of fruit each, hummus
with carrot sticks, and Garlicky Greens with three dinners a week (two with
fresh kale, one with frozen broccoli).

## 🧊 Freezer plan

One family bag is a meal for all three of us. A lunch tub is one adult
portion. Eat the oldest first.

| Week | In | Out | In the freezer after |
|------|----|-----|----------------------|
| 1 | 3 bags bolognese sauce, 8 tubs carrot soup | Fri: 1 bag bolognese. Sat lunch: 3 tubs soup | 2 bolognese, 5 tubs |
| 2 | 3 bags chilli | Fri: 1 bag bolognese. Sat lunch: 3 tubs soup | 1 bolognese, 3 chilli, 2 tubs |
| 3 | 3 bags black bean & squash stew (freeze without the mango) | Fri: 1 bag bolognese. Sat lunch: 1 bag chilli | 2 chilli, 3 black bean, 2 tubs |
| 4 | 3 bags Tuscan soup | Fri: 1 bag chilli. Sat lunch: 1 bag black bean | 1 chilli, 2 black bean, 3 Tuscan, 2 tubs |

The month ends with 6 family meals and 2 lunch tubs in the freezer, as a
buffer for a busy or poorly week. Next month, make one Sunday a
normal-sized cook so the freezer doesn't keep growing. You'll need about 12
three-portion boxes or bags and 8 single tubs.

## Daily Dozen check

- **Beans:** every lunch and dinner, plus hummus.
- **Berries, flaxseed, nuts and a whole grain:** the porridge.
- **Other fruit:** three pieces each. Apples and pears are at their best
  now.
- **Greens and cruciferous veg:** kale on three nights, plus cauliflower,
  cabbage, sprouts and chard. Add a handful of spinach, rocket or
  watercress to lunches to reach two servings of greens a day.
- **B12:** Greger recommends a supplement for adults. The NHS recommends
  vitamin drops for under-5s.
