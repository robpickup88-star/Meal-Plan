## Notes

- Cultivated mushrooms, garlic, onions, carrots and potatoes are available
  from UK stores most of the year, even in months not listed above.
- Red and white cabbage, kale and cavolo nero keep well and are usually
  British from autumn to spring.
- Good autumn and winter swaps: squash or swede for sweet potato; leeks,
  mushrooms and chard for peppers and courgettes; cauliflower, kale or
  Brussels sprouts for broccoli.
